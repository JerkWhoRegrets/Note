# Unresolved Questions

## Requirements-index verification boundary

- The physical Requirements Index contains 373 entries. Req Numbers 149, 150, 152, 211, 214, 230, 238, 252, 284, 316, 317, 329, 331, and 379 are absent from the index and are not unresolved protocol requirements. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, Requirements Index, pp. 275-285]

## Scheme or Vendor Dependency

- **DS legal operator.** The DS is operated in the Interoperability Domain, while the Payment System is only said to typically operate the DS CA. Confirm the applicable scheme's DS operator and trust/onboarding terms; do not infer that the CA operator and DS operator are necessarily identical. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 1.5, p. 21; sections 2.2.1-2.2.2, p. 32]
- **Payment System and DS rules.** AV algorithm and ECI are Payment System-specific, Attempts support is DS-specific, and DS programme rules may define logos/timeouts. Obtain the applicable scheme rules before ACS design is final. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 1.5, pp. 19 and 21; section 2.2.1, p. 32]
- **AReq/ARes read-timeout values are DS-defined.** The 3DS Server-to-DS and DS-to-ACS read durations are set by each DS implementation and may vary by DS; the Core specification supplies the start event and expiry action but no numeric duration. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 5.5.2-5.5.2.1, pp. 117-118, Reqs 228, 231, and 234]
- **Requestor Environment integration.** Private APIs, topology, and the division of control/provisioning between Requestor and 3DS Integrator are implementation-specific. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 1.5 and 2, pp. 18 and 29; sections 2.1.1-2.1.2, pp. 30-31]
- **Challenge cryptograms and limits.** ECI and Authentication Value behavior is defined by the applicable DS, and pages 43-58 do not set a numeric ACS maximum-challenges value. Obtain the applicable DS/Payment System rules before finalising ACS result generation and challenge-attempt policy. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.1, pp. 49 and 54, Reqs 31 and 61]
- **3RI versus recurring and merchant-initiated classifications.** Recurrent use cases are an example of 3RI, but Section 3.4 does not define scheme MIT, credential-on-file, or recurring scheduling rules and does not make those commercial terms synonyms for the 3RI device channel. Obtain the applicable scheme/business rules for mapping a merchant activity to a 3RI request. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 1 and 1.5, pp. 15 and 21; section 3.4, pp. 71-77]
- **3RI and Decoupled status/output rules.** Attempts support, several DS-produced failure statuses, ECI/Authentication Value generation, Transaction Status Reason inclusion, and DS logging detail remain specific to the applicable DS or Payment System. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.4, pp. 72-75 and 77, Reqs 271, 280-283, 292, 296, and 339, source footnote 6]
- **DS substitution and logging rules for ARes/RRes errors.** Sections 5.9.3.1-5.9.3.2 allow the DS to replace an error with ARes carrying a DS-defined Transaction Status, and Sections 5.9.3 and 5.9.10 defer transaction logging to DS rules. Obtain the applicable DS mapping and logging requirements. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 5.9.3-5.9.3.2 and 5.9.10, pp. 125-126 and 130]
- **Chapter 6 Payment System security dependencies.** Link a and a separate Requestor-Server integration defer to Payment System security requirements, while payment links e/f/g receive no additional EMV 3DS security requirements. Obtain the applicable scheme security, onboarding, mutual-authentication, and network profiles. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 6.1.1 and 6.1.5-6.1.7, pp. 134-137]
- **Certificate trust and lifecycle profiles are external.** Chapter 6 and Annex D identify DS-CA and commercial-CA trust roles, TLS key-size/cipher capabilities, and DS-CA client/server certificate provisioning, but do not state identity/SAN rules, accepted roots, issuance, path building, revocation, renewal, rotation, pinning, expiry, key custody, or compromise response. Obtain the applicable DS, Payment System, CA, SDK, issuer, and platform requirements. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 6.1.2-6.1.4, 6.1.8, and 6.2.3, pp. 135-143; Annex D, p. 273]
- **TLS 1.3 and later need an external interoperability profile.** Annex D permits TLS 1.2 or higher but names cipher suites only for TLS 1.2 and does not specify TLS 1.3+ cipher suites, groups, signature schemes, or negotiation policy. Obtain the applicable DS, Payment System, SDK, or vendor profile. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section D.1, p. 273]

Obtain the registered extension definition and applicable DS rules before accepting, producing, or interpreting extension content. Obtain the extension profile for binary encoding and define representation-preserving forwarding without presenting a JSON reserialization strategy as an EMV 3DS rule. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections A.6-A.6.3, pp. 234-237, Table A.7]
- **Batch 13 market/scheme conditions.** Payment System Image presence, NPA Transaction Status and Reason conditions, Transaction Type market mandates, DS-reserved values, and trusted-beneficiary/whitelist programme behavior are left to the applicable Payment System, DS, or market. Obtain those rules before turning optional or DS-conditional Table A.1 cells into validation policy. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section A.4, pp. 208 and 216-222, Table A.1]
- **Requestor authentication evidence profiles are not defined in v2.2.0.** A.7.3-A.7.4 leave `threeDSReqAuthData` and `threeDSReqPriorAuthData` undefined in detail even though examples mention federated-ID, FIDO attestation/signatures, and SRC assurance. Obtain the applicable method, DS, scheme, trust, encoding, verification, replay-binding, and failure-handling profile before interpreting the Strings. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections A.7.3-A.7.4, pp. 246 and 248, Tables A.10-A.11]

Obtain the applicable SDK, Payment System, DS, and issuer rules. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections A.7.9-A.7.10, pp. 255-256, Tables A.16-A.17]

## Issuer Policy Decision

- **Challenge policy.** ACS decides whether further cardholder interaction is required, while the Requestor decides whether to proceed. Risk, threshold, and regulatory triggers are examples in the overview, not a complete issuer policy. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 2.5.2, p. 36]
- **Logical ACS partitioning.** If ACS processing is divided across physical servers or card ranges, define how challenge state, interaction counts, and results remain consistent. The permission to partition is explicit; the consistency mechanism is not specified here. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 1.5 and 2.3.4, pp. 23 and 34]
- **Challenge method and passcode implementation.** Sections 3.1-3.2 require the ACS to determine a supported challenge method, populate challenge UI data, and check Cardholder-entered authentication data, but do not define issuer APIs for method discovery, passcode generation/delivery, or OTP verification. Those are issuer ACS implementation decisions unless another applicable specification or scheme rule governs them. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.1, pp. 50 and 53-54, Reqs 32 and 50-61]
- **OOB result integration.** The OOB communication method, authentication method, and Issuer/service-provider-to-ACS result interface are outside scope. Define how the ACS obtains and correlates the OOB result before Step 18 without presenting that interface as an EMV 3DS protocol API. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.2, pp. 57-58]
- **Decoupled Authentication Service integration.** Figure 3.4 shows dashed ACS-to-Authentication-Service request/response arrows outside the protocol, and Req 330 says how the Cardholder authentication decision is made is outside scope. Define the issuer authentication method, UX, service interface, result correlation, and operational timeout behavior without presenting them as EMV 3DS protocol messages or assuming that a 3DS Client participates. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.4, pp. 71 and 76, Figure 3.4, Req 330]
- **App OOB continuation depends on device/app context.** Figure 4.12 shows a manual Continue action, while Figure 4.6 and Section 3.2 say the SDK automatically posts CReq when the OOB app and Requestor App are on the same device and the App returns to foreground. Apply the appropriate scenario and do not require both actions. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.2, pp. 57-58; section 4.2.1.1, p. 85, Figure 4.6; section 4.2.3, p. 92, Figure 4.12]

## Implementation Decision

- **Transaction identifier lifecycle and mapping.** Table A.1 and the flow requirements define the 3DS Server, ACS, DS, and SDK Transaction IDs, including their generators/sources, UUID formats, and message-by-message presence. They do not define the IDs as aliases or derivable values, nor prescribe internal database keys, retention, or deletion. Keep them distinct and define those lifecycle controls locally. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.1, pp. 46-50, Reqs 3, 8, 16, 27, and 32; section A.4, pp. 157, 164, 198, and 214, Table A.1]
- **Payment Token component selection and integration.** Table A.1 now says the system in the 3-D Secure domain where de-tokenisation occurs - either the 3DS Server or DS - populates `payTokenInd` and `payTokenSource`. The Core specification still does not select which component performs de-tokenisation for a deployment or define the token service, cryptography, key management, or private integration. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 2.5.3, p. 36; section A.4, p. 200, Table A.1]
- **Transport binding details now include the Annex D profile, with remaining external dependencies.** Chapter 6 establishes server-authenticated TLS for Consumer/ACS Internet links, DS-CA mTLS for links b/c, and App JWE/JWS protection. Req 247 explicitly binds PReq to Section 6.1.2.1, although that subsection names only AReq/ARes. Annex D supplies TLS 1.2+, key-size floors, TLS 1.2 suite capabilities, P-256, and prohibited suites. The PReq/PRes subsection-scope tension, Browser/Requestor Annex D scope, TLS 1.3+ policy, certificate lifecycle, Payment System profiles, and deployment-specific proxy/onward protection remain to be resolved. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 5.6, p. 119, Req 247; sections 6.1-6.2, pp. 134-144; Annex D, p. 273]
- **Timeout configuration still has DS- and application-specific parts.** Section 5.5 now supplies the Browser initial-CReq and challenge-interface timers and the App CReq/CRes timers, but AReq/ARes read timeouts remain DS-defined and the OOB instruction to adjust the SDK timeout does not prescribe a distinct OOB value. Finalize configuration with the applicable DS requirements and SDK integration policy. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.2, p. 58; sections 5.5.1-5.5.2.2, pp. 116-118, Reqs 220-229, 231-237, 239, 312, and 343-344]
- **Final CRes and result-notification state handling.** Tables A.1/A.15 and B.5/B.8/B.9 define message membership and Transaction Status validity for final CRes, RReq, and RRes, subject to the separately recorded App/Browser B.4/B.5 conflicts. Internal persistence, atomic one-result transitions, retry state, and client-facing recovery after invalid/late RRes remain implementation decisions unless another cited rule governs them. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.1, pp. 54-56, Reqs 62-79; section A.4, pp. 211 and 216-220, Table A.1; section A.7.8, pp. 253-254, Table A.15; sections B.4-B.5 and B.8-B.9, pp. 265-266 and 268-269]
- **Browser cookie and storage policy.** Section 3.3 does not define cookie domains, SameSite behavior, lifetime, or browser storage. Table A.3 supplies optional opaque `threeDSSessionData` for correlating the final CRes POST with Requestor session handling, while the protocol transaction identifiers serve a different purpose. Define browser session policy without presenting it as an EMV 3DS rule. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.3, pp. 66-70, Reqs 117-140; section A.5.4, p. 226, Table A.3]
- **HTTP connection reuse is not specified.** The browser challenge uses one protocol CReq and one protocol CRes, but repeated ACS HTML/UI interactions may require several HTTP exchanges. The source does not require one HTTP request, TCP connection, or TLS connection to remain open across the challenge. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 2.4.3-2.4.4, pp. 34-35; section 3.3, pp. 67-70, Reqs 119-140; section 5.8.2, p. 123]
- **3RI final-result mapping is defined, while scheme mapping remains.** Table A.1 and Table B.8 now define RReq membership for Transaction Status, Transaction Status Reason, identifiers, and whitelist fields; Table A.15 constrains status validity. DS-specific NPA conditions, Attempts/ECI/Authentication Value behavior, and reserved-value mappings still require the applicable DS/Payment System rules. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.4, pp. 74-76, Reqs 290-292, 296, 328, 339, 349, and 353; section A.4, pp. 216-222, Table A.1; section A.7.8, pp. 253-254, Table A.15; section B.8, p. 268, Table B.8]
- **3RI pending-state retention.** Req 328 expressly requires the ACS to store the 3DS Server Transaction ID and DS Transaction ID for later RReq processing. The ACS also needs timer/result state as an implementation consequence, while Section 3.4 does not prescribe storage technology, a retention period, or whether the generated ACS Transaction ID must be persisted for RReq. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.4, pp. 74-77, Reqs 288, 328, 330, 341, and 349-354]
- **App UI accessibility, localization, and masking.** Sections 4.1-4.2 provide responsive/device-aware rendering rules, structured zones, and masked contact examples, but no normative accessibility standard, locale negotiation mechanism, translation/fallback policy, or masking algorithm. Obtain applicable SDK, DS, scheme, regulatory, and issuer design-system requirements without attributing them to pages 79-101. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 4.1-4.2, pp. 79-101, Reqs 160-169 and 362-378]
- **App challenge attempt, resend, and delivery-retry policy.** Section 5.5.2.2 supplies the SDK 10-second read timeout and a maximum timeout of at least five minutes, but the UI pages still do not set a numeric ACS maximum-attempt count, resend limit, or delivery-retry policy. Define those remaining values under applicable issuer/DS policy. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.1, p. 54, Req 61; sections 4.2.4.1 and 4.2.7.1, pp. 95 and 101, Reqs 153 and 159; section 5.5.2.2, p. 118, Reqs 237, 239, and 312]
- **Browser Cancel and Resend behavior.** Browser figures show Cancel and a cross-channel example shows Resend Code, but Section 4.3 does not define browser action field names, a form schema, resend delivery/rate limits, or cancel confirmation. Abandonment is reported through RReq; do not reuse the App repeated-CReq action fields as Browser requirements. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 3.3, pp. 67-68, Reqs 122-126; sections 4.1 and 4.3.3, pp. 81 and 104-108, Figures 4.2 and 4.19-4.23]
- **Browser UI accessibility, localization, masking, and hardening.** Sections 4.3-4.4 specify device/window formatting, limited information controls, TLS, browser-compatible CA use, authentication-only redirects/resources, and JavaScript fallback, but not an accessibility standard, locale mechanism, masking algorithm, CSP/cookie/origin policy, or sensitive-input handling policy. Obtain applicable DS, scheme, issuer, regulatory, and web-security requirements without attributing them to pages 102-110. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 3.3, 4.3-4.4, and 5.8.2, pp. 67, 102-109, and 123, Reqs 183, 266, 307, 324, and 380-384]
- **No general duplicate-message replay contract is defined.** The specification defines duplicate data element (`204`), duplicate/unrecognised Transaction ID (`301`), and already processed CReq (`305`) conditions, but does not define a universal duplicate key, cached-response replay, or uncertain-delivery recovery algorithm. Define idempotent state transitions under applicable DS/SDK rules without presenting internal locking or replay as an EMV 3DS requirement. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 5.1.6, 5.5, 5.6, and 5.9, pp. 114-132, Reqs 212, 222, 225, 229, 233, 236, 240, 243, and 303; section A.5.5, pp. 228-229, Table A.4]
- **Browser late HTML submissions are not assigned a protocol response.** After the 10-minute Browser interface timer expires, ACS sends RReq and CRes and the challenge completes, but Sections 5.1-5.8 do not specify the error code, page, or response for a later Cardholder form submission. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 5.5.1, pp. 116-117, Reqs 226-227 and 343-344]
- **PRes read timeout and late-response behavior are not specified.** Section 5.6 defines PReq connection retry, refresh cadence, and validation, but gives no PRes read-timeout duration, uncertain-delivery duplicate rule, or behavior for a PRes arriving after local refresh abandonment. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 5.6, pp. 119-121, Reqs 246-251]
- **Error HTTP status and recovery behavior are not specified.** General transport defines request POST and corresponding-response reply behavior, but Section 5.9 and Table A.4 do not assign HTTP status codes, retry-after behavior, cached-response replay, or a universal recovery action for Error Messages. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section 5.1.1, p. 111, Reqs 184 and 189; section 5.9, pp. 124-132; section A.5.5, pp. 227-230, Table A.4]
- **TLS termination and onward protection are architectural decisions.** Chapter 6 specifies endpoint link properties but not reverse proxies, gateways, service meshes, TLS offload, or re-encryption. Any terminator can see TLS-only application plaintext and needs separately defined trust, key, logging, and onward-protection controls. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 6.1.1-6.1.4 and 6.1.8, pp. 134-137]
- **App secure-channel restart and cluster state are not specified.** The SDK and ACS must derive keys, zero and compare direction counters, increment them, and fail on wrap, but pages 141-144 do not define persistence, multi-node synchronisation, process-restart recovery, retention, or deletion timing after completion. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, sections 6.2.3.2-6.2.4.4, pp. 141-144]
- **No Chapter 6 control is standalone compliance proof.** PCI DSS, scheme certification, privacy/data-protection obligations, issuer policy, secure-development controls, HSM use, logging/redaction, vulnerability management, and regulatory authentication rules remain outside pages 133-144. Do not infer that TLS, mTLS, or JWE alone satisfies them. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, chapter 6, pp. 133-144]
- **A.8 equal display orders need a local deterministic layout rule.** Table A.18 gives the same top-down order to both images (`1`), Challenge Information Text and its indicator (`3`), and two different order-`5` fields, but supplies no secondary ordering algorithm. Preserve the specified zones/order and use applicable SDK/design guidance without presenting a tie-breaker as an A.8 requirement. [EMV 3DS v2.2.0, section A.8, pp. 257-258, Table A.18]
